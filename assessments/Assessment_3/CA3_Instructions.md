## Assessment Tasks

Students are given simulated Sanger data (generated uniquely from their Student ID).

### Task 1: Calculate Mean Quality Score (15 pts)
- Load the data
- Calculate mean Phred quality score

### Task 2: Count High-Quality Bases (15 pts)
- Count bases with Q ≥ 20
- Calculate percentage

### Task 3: Apply Quality Trimming (15 pts)
- Find trim positions (Q ≥ 20 threshold)
- Calculate trimmed sequence length

### Task 4: Report Trimmed Sequence (15 pts)
- Extract the trimmed sequence
- Report the new mean quality

### AI Interaction (20 pts)
Document prompts and responses.

### Reflection (20 pts)
Answer reflection questions.
